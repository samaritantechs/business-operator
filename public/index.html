About Samaritan Industrial

Samaritan Industrial helps shops and vendors leave the counterbook behind — recording sales, tracking stock and cash, and sending reports digitally. And now it gives every business a free storefront in our shared marketplace, so more customers can find what you sell.

📝

Goodbye Counterbooks

Record every sale digitally in seconds. No handwriting, no lost pages, no carbon paper.

🛍️

A Free Storefront

Your products appear in the marketplace with photos, prices and your contact — at no cost.

📊

Reports & Insights

Daily, weekly and monthly reports plus stock and cash tracking, ready whenever you need them.

👥

Your Whole Team

Sellers record from their own devices while owners keep full oversight in one dashboard.
